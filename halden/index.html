Notes from the workshop

  1. We tested every source from 1800 K candle-warm to 4000 K office-white. Below 2400 K a room starts to feel like it is closing; above 3000 K skin turns grey.

    2700 K is the colour of a 60-watt bulb at the end of a hallway. Every lamp in N°7 starts there, and dims warmer the way filament used to.

  2. Opal glass is clear glass with a little bone ash folded in. Too little and the bulb shows through as a hot spot; too much and the sphere goes dull.

    Mr. Kang's team blows forty Orbe spheres a season. About one in five is set aside for a bubble you would never notice, except under light.

  3. We stopped lacquering brass in 2022. Untreated, it darkens to the colour of tea within a year, faster where hands touch it.

    If you want it bright again, a cloth and a little vinegar will do it. Most owners don't.

Letters, four times a year. New pieces, studio hours, nothing else.
